Frequently asked questions

Are the calculated values safe to enter directly into my DCS or controller?
No. Every calculator in this app is meant to give you a starting point to check your own work against — not a final answer to apply without review. Always verify results against your facility's engineering standards and follow your site's Management of Change (MOC) process before implementing any change. See the Terms of Use for the full statement on this.

Does the app collect or store any of my data?
No. The app makes no network requests and has no backend server — everything you type into it stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Which vessel/loop configurations does the Level Calculator support?
Dry leg below-tap, remote seal, both with interface-level variants, and steam drum level with saturated-steam density compensation.

Which process models does the PID Tuning Calculator support?
First Order Plus Dead Time (FODT) and Integrating (Ramp) process models, using a two-stage method: characterize the process from a step test, then choose a desired closed-loop speed.

I found a bug or have a suggestion — how do I report it?
Email contact@strategicplatformpartners.com with as much detail as you can (what screen, what you entered, what you expected vs. what happened). This is a great way to help improve the app for everyone.
