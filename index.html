Privacy Policy for Basic CSE Toolkit

Effective date: August 4, 2026

Basic CSE Toolkit does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter into the app stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Information We Collect

None. Specifically:

  • No accounts or sign-in. The app has no login, registration, or user profile of any kind.
  • No data leaves your device. The app makes no network requests and has no backend server. Tag numbers, calculation inputs, MOC (Management of Change) notes, and any other values you type into the app are never transmitted anywhere.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app does not use analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, advertising identifiers, or any other third-party tracking tools.
  • No third-party services. The app does not integrate with any third-party data processors, ad networks, or external APIs.
  • No device permissions. The app does not request access to your camera, location, contacts, photos, microphone, or any other sensitive device data or hardware.

Data Storage

Any values you enter (tag numbers, calculation inputs, MOC records, etc.) are held only in the app's temporary in-memory state while you're using it. Nothing is written to persistent storage on your device, and nothing is saved once you close the app or navigate away from a screen.

Children's Privacy

This app is a professional engineering reference and calculation tool, not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does collect or store data — this page will be updated and the effective date above will reflect the change.
